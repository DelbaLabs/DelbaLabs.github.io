Common questions

I can't move several cards at once. The number of cards you can move together depends on your free cells and empty columns: (free cells + 1) × 2 for each empty column. This is the standard FreeCell rule, and every deal is solvable with it.

I lost my coins or purchases after reinstalling. Progress is stored in your iCloud (key-value storage) and merged when you reopen the game. Purchases: Settings → Shop → Restore purchases.

How do I change my ad choices? Settings → Privacy and ad choices (shown where the law requires it).
