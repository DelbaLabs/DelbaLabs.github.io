DelbaLabs Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 6 October 2026.

This policy covers mobile apps and games published by DelbaLabs and related support and website interactions. The data controller for processing determined by DelbaLabs is Alessandro Vincenzo Del Balzo, independent developer operating as DelbaLabs. Privacy contact: delbalabs@proton.me.

Features vary by app, version, platform and region. A service described here is used only where it is actually integrated and enabled. This policy does not mean that every app contains ads, analytics, crash reporting, purchases, accounts or cloud storage. The app's notices, privacy controls, store disclosures and any app-specific policy provide further details. Existing app-specific policies remain available; their narrower descriptions of actual features continue to apply. This general policy does not, by itself, activate new collection or change an app's existing practices.

1. Data that may be processed

  • Local app data: progress, scores, settings, preferences, unlocked items and files you choose to open or process. Local processing does not itself send those files or data to DelbaLabs. Cloud backup or synchronisation, if offered, is described in the app-specific notice; device or store backup settings may also apply.
  • Technical and device information: IP address, device model, operating system, app version, language, approximate region inferred from IP, network information, timestamps, installation or app-instance identifiers, and advertising identifiers where available and permitted.
  • Usage and advertising information: sessions, screens or gameplay events, feature use, ad impressions, clicks, rewarded-ad completion and consent choices, where relevant services are enabled.
  • Diagnostics: crash traces, errors, performance measurements, device state and associated installation identifiers, if crash reporting or diagnostics is used.
  • Purchase information: product identifier, purchase status, transaction or receipt information and subscription entitlement needed to provide or restore a purchase.
  • Support communications: your email address, message and attachments you voluntarily send. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Sources include your device and app interactions, information you submit, and relevant service providers or stores. Not all information is received directly by DelbaLabs: integrated providers may collect data through their software. We do not request precise location, contacts, health information or other sensitive data as a general portfolio feature. Any app that requires additional permissions or materially different processing needs a specific notice before that processing.

2. Purposes and legal bases

We process relevant data to operate requested features, save progress, provide and restore purchases, respond to support, maintain reliability and security, prevent abuse, understand app use where analytics is enabled, and deliver and measure ads where advertising is enabled.

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, the basis depends on the purpose: performance of a contract for requested services and purchase entitlements; legitimate interests for proportionate support, reliability, security and fraud prevention, balanced against your rights; legal obligations where required; and consent for optional analytics, advertising personalisation, device storage/access or tracking where legally required. A legitimate interest does not replace consent when consent is required. Under Swiss law, applicable transparency, consent and other processing requirements also apply. Contact us for the basis relevant to a particular app or processing activity.

3. Advertising, including rewarded ads

Apps may use Google AdMob for banners, interstitials and optional rewarded ads. Google and any advertising partners actually enabled may process technical identifiers, IP-derived approximate location and ad interactions to deliver ads, measure performance, limit repetition and detect fraud. Personalised advertising may involve profiling of interests, subject to your choices and applicable law. Actual vendors are identified through the app's consent interface or app-specific disclosure where applicable.

Choosing to watch a rewarded ad is a choice to receive the advertised in-app reward; it is not automatically consent to personalised advertising or tracking. Reward verification may involve completion events or transaction identifiers. Declining privacy consent can affect available ad types or rewarded-ad availability. Non-personalised ads can still process data and use device storage; they are not necessarily anonymous or exempt from consent requirements.

4. Google Firebase, Analytics and diagnostics

Where enabled, Google Analytics for Firebase may process app events, usage and device or app-instance information to help understand engagement and improve an app. Optional collection and any advertising-related use are subject to applicable consent requirements and the app's configuration. An app with no analytics does not start using analytics merely because this policy mentions it.

Where enabled, Firebase Crashlytics or performance diagnostics may process crash traces, errors, technical device information and installation identifiers to investigate failures. Information available to DelbaLabs depends on the tools and configuration actually used. We do not intend to include user documents, payment card details or support messages in analytics or crash logs. Other Firebase services, accounts or server-side storage require appropriate app-specific disclosures if introduced.

5. Store purchases and cloud features

In-app purchases and subscriptions, if offered, are processed by Apple App Store, Google Play or the relevant store under its own terms and privacy policy. DelbaLabs does not receive your full payment card details. The app or DelbaLabs may receive purchase or entitlement information to unlock content, verify transactions, manage subscriptions or restore purchases. Store records are managed by the store.

Some apps may keep progress in your private iCloud storage or use platform backup. Access, retention and deletion depend on the feature and your platform settings. Uninstalling an app may remove local data but does not necessarily remove cloud backups, store records or data already sent to a provider.

6. Consent and privacy choices in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

Where required, relevant apps request consent before consent-dependent processing, including access to device storage and advertising personalisation, using Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) or another appropriate consent interface. Choices and vendor information depend on the app and region. You may refuse or withdraw consent through the app's privacy or ad-choice controls where offered, or contact us if you cannot find them. Withdrawal does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal.

Device advertising controls are available in iOS or Android settings. If an iOS app performs tracking covered by Apple's App Tracking Transparency rules, the required permission is separate from privacy consent; no such tracking should take place without the required permission. Accepting this policy or simply using an app is not consent to optional processing. Contact us about missing controls or unexpected data collection.

7. United States privacy rights — Do Not Sell or Share / Privacy choices

Depending on your state and whether the relevant law applies to DelbaLabs, you may have rights to know or access personal information, receive a portable copy, correct it, delete it, and opt out of its sale, sharing for cross-context behavioural advertising or use for targeted advertising. Some laws provide rights concerning sensitive information or profiling with legal or similarly significant effects. We do not use app data to make such significant automated decisions or seek sensitive data for unrelated purposes.

We do not sell personal information for money. However, disclosure of identifiers and ad-interaction data to advertising providers may be considered a “sale” or “sharing” under some state laws, depending on the actual configuration. The potentially relevant categories are identifiers, internet/app activity, device information and approximate geolocation, shared with enabled advertising providers for advertising and measurement. Diagnostic providers, stores, hosting and support providers receive the categories relevant to their services. We do not assert that all of these disclosures are sales or that any specific statutory threshold is met.

To request an opt-out: use the relevant app's privacy/ad-choice controls or email delbalabs@proton.me with the subject “Privacy opt-out request” and the app name. You may request access, correction or deletion through the same address. Where applicable, recognised universal opt-out signals, such as Global Privacy Control, must be respected for processing to which they apply; a browser signal may not reach a native app, so also use the app's controls or contact us. This static page does not itself change the settings of an installed app.

We will handle requests under applicable law, verify identity only as reasonably necessary, and explain any lawful refusal. An authorised agent may act for you with appropriate proof of authority. Where a state provides an appeal right, reply to our decision requesting an appeal; you may also contact your state regulator. You will not be discriminated against for exercising applicable privacy rights. Advertising-dependent rewards may depend on ad availability, rather than acceptance of personalised advertising.

8. Children and young users

Unless an app explicitly states otherwise with a dedicated child-appropriate notice, DelbaLabs apps are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. A store age rating describes content suitability and is not, by itself, permission for data collection. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in breach of applicable law.

Higher local ages and parental-consent rules may apply, including ages 13–16 for consent to certain online services in the EEA. Where child status is known, child-related restrictions must apply, including limits on personalised advertising and tracking. Where applicable US law requires it, sale or sharing of information of known users under 16 requires affirmative authorisation, including verifiable parental authorisation for those under 13. If an app is directed to children or needs parental consent, a specific notice and appropriate safeguards are required before such processing. A parent or guardian concerned about a child's data should contact us so we can investigate and delete or restrict data as required.

9. Recipients and third-party policies

Relevant data may be processed by the service providers actually used for advertising, analytics, diagnostics, store payments, hosting, cloud functionality and support. Providers may act on our behalf or as independent controllers depending on the service and terms. We may also disclose necessary information to comply with law, protect rights or address fraud, or in a business transfer subject to applicable safeguards and notice.

  • Google / AdMob: Google Privacy Policy, data from apps using Google services, advertising.
  • Firebase / Analytics / Crashlytics: Firebase privacy and security, Analytics retention controls.
  • Apple App Store / iCloud: Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play: Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub Pages: GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our website and apps may request public configuration files or app listings from GitHub Pages. Ordinary network requests expose IP address and request metadata to the host. This privacy page contains no embedded ads, analytics scripts or external fonts. External websites and stores have their own practices; additional enabled ad vendors must be disclosed in the relevant app or consent interface.

10. Retention

Data is retained only as long as needed for the purpose, considering the app configuration, service-provider settings, security needs and legal obligations. Local progress normally remains until cleared by you or removed with the app; backups may persist separately. Support correspondence is kept while needed to resolve the request and any related dispute. Purchase or entitlement records may be retained while necessary to provide the purchase and meet accounting or legal requirements.

Analytics retention depends on configured controls; diagnostic and advertising data follow the relevant service's retention and deletion rules. We do not promise a single retention period across all apps or providers. Ask us for the retention settings of a particular app. Data under our control is deleted or anonymised when no longer needed, subject to legal holds and backup deletion cycles; genuinely anonymous aggregates may be retained.

11. International transfers and security

Providers may process data outside your country, including in the United States, where privacy laws may differ. Where required, transfers rely on applicable safeguards, such as adequacy decisions, valid and applicable Data Privacy Framework participation, standard contractual clauses, UK transfer mechanisms and Swiss adaptations. The appropriate mechanism depends on the recipient and service; contact us for information about relevant safeguards. This policy does not claim that DelbaLabs itself is certified under a transfer framework.

We use reasonable measures appropriate to the information and processing, including limiting access and using secure service features where available. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Avoid sending unnecessary confidential data in support messages.

12. Your rights and how to contact us

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection, including objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdraw consent. Write to delbalabs@proton.me, identifying the app and your request. We will help with data under our control, including data held by processors, and explain when a request also needs to go to a store or independent provider. We may need limited information to locate records or verify identity; do not send identity documents unless necessary and requested. We respond within applicable legal deadlines and explain any permitted extension or exception.

You may complain to your local data protection authority, including Italy's Garante, the UK's ICO or Switzerland's FDPIC, as appropriate. You do not need to contact us first to exercise a statutory complaint right.

13. Updates and app-specific information

We will update this page and its date when our practices change. Significant changes require appropriate notice and new consent where legally required. Future features are not authorised merely by their inclusion in this policy. The stable policy URL is https://delbalabs.github.io/privacy/.

Existing app-specific pages: Glowtrace / Nodelight, Sparkfall, Celldeck. If a notice seems inconsistent or you need exact details for an app/version, contact us.

Informativa sulla privacy di DelbaLabs

In vigore e aggiornata il 6 ottobre 2026.

Questa informativa riguarda le app e i giochi mobili pubblicati da DelbaLabs e le relative interazioni con assistenza e sito. Il titolare dei trattamenti determinati da DelbaLabs è Alessandro Vincenzo Del Balzo, sviluppatore indipendente che opera come DelbaLabs. Contatto privacy: delbalabs@proton.me.

Le funzionalità variano per app, versione, piattaforma e regione. I servizi descritti si applicano soltanto se effettivamente integrati e attivati. Non tutte le app contengono pubblicità, analisi, diagnostica, acquisti, account o archiviazione cloud. Gli avvisi dell'app, i controlli privacy, le dichiarazioni dello store e le informative specifiche forniscono ulteriori dettagli. Le informative già esistenti restano disponibili e continuano a descrivere le funzionalità effettive delle singole app. Questa informativa generale non attiva nuove raccolte né modifica da sola le pratiche esistenti.

1. Dati che possono essere trattati

  • Dati locali: progressi, punteggi, impostazioni, preferenze, contenuti sbloccati e file che scegli di aprire o elaborare. L'elaborazione locale non comporta di per sé l'invio dei file o dei dati a DelbaLabs. Backup e sincronizzazione cloud, se offerti, sono descritti nell'informativa specifica; possono applicarsi anche le impostazioni di backup del dispositivo o dello store.
  • Dati tecnici e del dispositivo: indirizzo IP, modello, sistema operativo, versione dell'app, lingua, regione approssimativa ricavata dall'IP, informazioni di rete, orari, identificativi di installazione o istanza dell'app e identificativi pubblicitari, se disponibili e consentiti.
  • Utilizzo e pubblicità: sessioni, schermate o eventi di gioco, uso delle funzioni, visualizzazioni e clic sugli annunci, completamento di video con premio e scelte di consenso, se i relativi servizi sono attivi.
  • Diagnostica: tracce degli arresti anomali, errori, misurazioni delle prestazioni, stato del dispositivo e identificativi di installazione, se tali strumenti sono utilizzati.
  • Acquisti: identificativo del prodotto, stato dell'acquisto, informazioni di transazione o ricevuta e diritto all'abbonamento necessari per fornire o ripristinare un acquisto.
  • Assistenza: indirizzo email, messaggio e allegati che invii volontariamente. Evita di inviare dati sensibili non necessari.

Le fonti sono il dispositivo, le interazioni con l'app, le informazioni che fornisci e i fornitori o store pertinenti. DelbaLabs non riceve necessariamente tutti questi dati direttamente: i fornitori integrati possono raccoglierli tramite i propri strumenti. La raccolta di posizione precisa, contatti, dati sanitari o altri dati sensibili non è una funzionalità generale del portfolio. Un'app che richieda ulteriori permessi o trattamenti sostanzialmente diversi necessita di un avviso specifico prima di tali trattamenti.

2. Finalità e basi giuridiche

I dati pertinenti servono a fornire le funzionalità richieste, salvare i progressi, fornire e ripristinare acquisti, rispondere all'assistenza, mantenere affidabilità e sicurezza, prevenire abusi, comprendere l'uso dell'app se le analisi sono attive e mostrare e misurare gli annunci se la pubblicità è attiva.

Quando si applicano GDPR o UK GDPR, la base dipende dalla finalità: esecuzione del contratto per servizi richiesti e diritti di acquisto; legittimo interesse per assistenza proporzionata, affidabilità, sicurezza e prevenzione delle frodi, bilanciato con i tuoi diritti; obblighi di legge; consenso per analisi facoltative, personalizzazione pubblicitaria, accesso o archiviazione sul dispositivo e tracciamento quando richiesto. Il legittimo interesse non sostituisce il consenso quando questo è necessario. In Svizzera si applicano anche i pertinenti requisiti di trasparenza, consenso e trattamento. Puoi contattarci per conoscere la base relativa a una specifica app o attività.

3. Pubblicità e video con premio

Le app possono usare Google AdMob per banner, annunci interstiziali e video con premio facoltativi. Google e gli eventuali partner effettivamente attivati possono trattare identificativi tecnici, posizione approssimativa ricavata dall'IP e interazioni pubblicitarie per mostrare e misurare annunci, limitarne la ripetizione e individuare frodi. La personalizzazione può comportare profilazione degli interessi, secondo le tue scelte e la legge. I fornitori effettivi sono indicati nell'interfaccia di consenso o nell'informativa specifica quando applicabile.

Guardare un video con premio significa scegliere la relativa ricompensa nell'app: non equivale automaticamente al consenso alla pubblicità personalizzata o al tracciamento. La verifica del premio può utilizzare eventi di completamento o identificativi di transazione. Il rifiuto del consenso può incidere sui tipi di annunci o sulla disponibilità dei premi. Anche gli annunci non personalizzati possono trattare dati e usare memoria del dispositivo: non sono necessariamente anonimi né esenti dai requisiti di consenso.

4. Google Firebase, Analytics e diagnostica

Se attivo, Google Analytics for Firebase può trattare eventi dell'app, dati di utilizzo e informazioni del dispositivo o dell'istanza dell'app per comprendere l'utilizzo e migliorare il prodotto. Se attivi, Firebase Crashlytics o strumenti di diagnostica delle prestazioni possono trattare tracce degli arresti, errori, dati tecnici e identificativi di installazione per investigare problemi. I dati disponibili a DelbaLabs dipendono dagli strumenti e dalla configurazione utilizzati. Non intendiamo inserire documenti degli utenti, dati delle carte o messaggi di assistenza nei registri di analisi o diagnostica. Altri servizi Firebase, account o archiviazione su server richiedono informative specifiche adeguate se introdotti.

5. Acquisti tramite store e funzioni cloud

DelbaLabs non riceve i dati completi delle carte di pagamento. Alcune app possono conservare progressi nel tuo spazio iCloud privato o utilizzare backup della piattaforma. Accesso, conservazione e cancellazione dipendono dalla funzione e dalle impostazioni. Non vendiamo dati personali in cambio di denaro.
